Image exploration, 2026-11-16

One image draft explored a 2 by 2 sheet of four original flat marks on warm white.
The palette was navy and deep teal, with bold ribbons and clear negative spaces.

A: two interlinked house-shaped loops with alternating over/under breaks.
B: an open square spiral around a roof and small courtyard window.
C: two soft cloud ribbons around a house opening.
D: two offset capsule loops around a roof and window.

The prompt required different silhouettes and construction, not color variants.
It excluded any copy or trace of an existing logo, a sixfold knot, a rosette,
a hexagonal swirl, vendor branding, prompt symbols, text, gradients and glow.

The image draft supplied visual exploration only. Its pixels are not shipped.
The final SVGs use independently drawn paths in build_marks.py. The wide logo
compositions are redrawn, rather than stretched square marks. Transparent SVG
masks create the weave gaps. Inkscape renders the PNGs; ImageMagick makes the
native small samples and removes metadata. build_comparison.py composes the
one-page sheet from those actual exported assets, in the first sheet's layout.
