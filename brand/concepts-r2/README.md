# Four woven directions

[Open the comparison sheet](choices.png) at 100% for the native small samples. Each row follows the first comparison's layout: square icon and landscape logo, on light and dark backgrounds. Icons are 256 × 256. Logos are 320 × 128. Small samples are 32 × 32 and 80 × 32. The landscape marks use their own wide geometry, without a wordmark.

A, Roofweave: two house-shaped loops cross over and under. The paired roofs suggest a home and its helper. The two transparent breaks make the weave visible without a background-colored patch.

B, Courtyard: an open square spiral turns inward around a roof and a small window. It suggests a useful path into a home. Its angular silhouette gives it the clearest difference from a round knot.

C, Cloudloom: two soft cloud loops surround an open house. The cloud carries the family resemblance; the prominent roof gives the mark its own subject. The open doorway stays visible at 32 px.

D, Orbit: two offset capsule loops circle a roof and window. It suggests a helper around the home. The low silhouette differs from the upright houses and the square spiral.

These are four ideas, not color variants. Each has light and dark colors with identical geometry. All masters use original paths, two solid colors and transparent backgrounds. They contain no traced vendor mark, sixfold knot, prompt symbol, product name or wordmark. The proposal leaves the first concepts and installed images intact. No direction is selected here.

The [OpenAI design guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) prohibit similar third-party logos and implied endorsement. These proposals use houses, asymmetric loops and a square spiral instead of the Blossom's sixfold construction or the Codex cloud-and-prompt symbol. Review the full-size and small samples before selecting a direction. The family echo is the broad visual language, not borrowed geometry.

The [Home Assistant image rules](https://contributors.home-assistant.io/core/integrations/brands/) describe transparent PNG icons and landscape logos. This folder holds proposals only. The selected direction still needs its approved integration and store exports.

The [exploration record](sources/generation-prompt.txt) explains the image draft. The final assets come from [editable SVG geometry](sources/build_marks.py), not the draft's pixels. Run `bash brand/concepts-r2/sources/export.sh` from the repository root to reproduce all masters, PNGs and the sheet. It uses Python, Inkscape and ImageMagick. The comparison labels use DejaVu Sans; the marks use no fonts.
